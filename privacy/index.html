Privacy Policy

Tree Wreckers and the Tree Wreckers Demo, by Boca Games LLC

Effective October 9, 2026

The short version

  • The game sends us play data only if you say yes, and you can change your answer in Settings at any time.
  • Play data is how your jobs go, any errors, and your computer's hardware and settings. It doesn't include your name, email address or Steam account details.
  • Unity Gaming Services handles play data for us. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • In co-op, voice chat goes live to the other players in your game, and you can turn it off in Settings. It isn't recorded, and it's never sent to us.

1. Who we are

Boca Games LLC ("we", "us") makes Tree Wreckers and its free demo (together, "the game"). We're responsible for the play data the game collects: under European data protection law, we're its controller. For anything about your privacy, email bocagamesllc@gmail.com.

2. Play data, only if you agree

The first time you start the game, it asks whether you'll share play data. If you choose SHARE, the game sends the following:

  • How your jobs go: when a job starts and how long it takes to make your first cut; each job's result (pay, rating, the number of damage charges, minutes taken, cut attempts); when a rope snaps; which button you press on the demo's final screen; and whether you hosted or joined a co-op game.
  • Errors: error messages from the game, shortened, and with your Windows user folder taken out of any file paths.
  • Your setup: your graphics card and its memory, the graphics preset you use, and whether you're playing the demo or the full game.
  • Added by Unity's software: a random ID for your copy of the game (it isn't linked to your name or your Steam account), session IDs and timestamps, the game's version and platform, your computer's model, processor, graphics card, memory, screen size, operating system version and language setting.
  • Your IP address: Unity's servers receive it when data is sent, and use it to estimate your country.

If you choose NO THANKS, or don't answer, none of this is sent.

What we use it for: finding and fixing bugs and crashes; seeing which parts of the game are too hard or confusing (for example, how long the first cut takes); choosing graphics settings that run well on players' computers; and seeing how the demo ends. We look at play data across all players, not to follow any one player.

Legal basis (for players in the European Economic Area and the UK): your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

3. Unity Gaming Services

Play data is processed for us by Unity Technologies through Unity Gaming Services Analytics. Unity handles it on our behalf, under its data processing terms, and may process it in the United States and other countries. Unity keeps it for 13 months and then deletes it. You can read Unity's own privacy policy at unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

4. Changing your mind, and deleting your data

In the game, go to SETTINGS → SHARE PLAY DATA. Turning it off stops play data being sent straight away, and asks Unity to delete the play data it holds from your copy of the game. You can turn it back on whenever you like.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted. Play data isn't linked to your name, so we may need your help to find yours (for example, a file from the game's folder on your computer).

5. Steam and playing with friends

Steam. If you play through Steam, Valve handles your Steam account, your friends list, your purchases and the connection between players. The game uses Steam to make game lobbies, send invites and connect you to your friends. Connections between players go through Valve's relay network, so the players you play with don't see your IP address. We don't receive or keep your Steam account details. Valve's privacy policy applies to Steam: store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement. Players in your lobby can see your Steam profile name, as Steam shows it.

Voice chat. In a co-op game, the game turns on your computer's default microphone and sends your voice, as you speak, to the other players in your game, through the host's computer and, on Steam, Valve's network. It's played to them and then discarded. It isn't recorded or stored, and it's never sent to us or to Unity. When you play alone, the microphone stays off. To play with others without your microphone, turn off VOICE CHAT in the game's Settings: then your microphone stays off and you don't hear the other players' voices either.

6. Files on your computer

The game saves your progress and settings on your computer. It also keeps a log of each session's play (the same kinds of events as play data) in its folder under AppData\LocalLow\Boca Games (Tree Wreckers for the game, Tree Wreckers Demo for the free demo), along with Unity's log and crash files. These stay on your computer. They're only sent to us if you choose to send them, for example in a playtest we've invited you to. Delete them whenever you like.

7. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and what you send only to reply to you and help, and we keep it only as long as we need it for that.

8. What we don't do

We don't sell or rent personal information. We don't share it for targeted advertising, and the game shows no ads. We never see your payment details: Steam handles purchases.

9. Children

The game isn't meant for children under 13, and Steam accounts are for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age for consent where you live. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the UK, California or other US states), you may have the right to:

  • get a copy of your personal information;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • object to its use or restrict it;
  • take it elsewhere;
  • withdraw your consent.

California residents: we don't sell or share personal information, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights.

To use any of these rights, email us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

Play data is sent over encrypted connections. We keep access to it limited to the people who work on the game.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If the change is significant, we'll also say so in the game or on the game's Steam page.
